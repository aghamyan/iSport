ISPORT — GAME RULES
====================
Compiled directly from the codebase (matches, rivalries, championships, and
the player ranking system). Betting/wagering rules are intentionally
excluded from this document.


1. FRIENDLY MATCHES (1v1)
--------------------------
- A match must be between two different players (a player cannot play
  themselves).
- Lifecycle: a match starts as PENDING when created, then becomes
  CONFIRMED once the away player enters the final score.
- Both scores must be present before a match can move to CONFIRMED.
- Edit window: once confirmed, either player can still edit the score or
  notes for 4 hours after confirmation. After that the edit is locked for
  regular players — this is enforced at the database level (a trigger
  rejects the update once the 4-hour window has passed). Admins are exempt
  from this window and can edit confirmed matches at any time.
- Only a PENDING (not yet confirmed) match can be deleted by its players.
  Admins can delete matches directly regardless of status.


2. PERSONAL RIVALRIES (head-to-head series)
--------------------------------------------
- Only one rivalry can exist per unique pair of players — you can't create
  a duplicate rivalry with the same opponent.
- When starting a rivalry, the creator sets a "Score to win a session"
  target (any whole number from 1 to 999; default 5).
- Recording a session enforces a race-to-target format:
    • The winner's score must equal exactly the target number.
    • The loser's score must be below the target.
    • Scores cannot tie at or above the target (a tie is only valid when
      both scores are below the target — that session counts as a draw).
  (This validation happens client-side when recording a session.)
- Recording a session is immediate — there is no separate "confirm" step
  like friendly matches have. Either participant (or an admin) can record
  a session, and it instantly updates the running win/draw tally.
- The rivalry series itself is unlimited and never auto-completes — it's
  simply a running tally of wins/draws over time between the two players
  (an earlier design capped the series at a "best of N" and auto-declared
  a series winner; the current version removed that cap).
- A "Rivalry Champion" badge exists in the schema for winning a best-of
  series, but since series no longer auto-complete, it is not
  automatically awarded under the current rules.


3. CHAMPIONSHIPS / TOURNAMENTS
--------------------------------
Three formats are supported, chosen by the admin at creation:

  a) ROUND ROBIN
     - Requires at least 2 players.
     - Runs 1–10 cycles (admin-chosen). Each cycle is a fresh round-robin
       where every player faces every other player once.
     - Home/away venue alternates by cycle parity: odd cycles use the
       original pairing order, even cycles reverse it — so across every
       two-cycle block, each pair plays each other once at each venue.
     - Cycles are generated one at a time (not all upfront); the next
       cycle cannot be generated once the championship's total cycle cap
       has been reached.

  b) GROUP KNOCKOUT
     - Requires at least 4 players.
     - Players are split into Group A and Group B (if the count is odd,
       Group A gets the extra player).
     - Each group plays its own round-robin for the configured number of
       cycles.
     - Top 2 finishers from each group advance to the semi-finals.
     - Semi-final pairings (Champions-League style):
         Tie 1: Group A's 1st vs Group B's 2nd
         Tie 2: Group B's 1st vs Group A's 2nd
     - Group matches must all be completed before semi-finals can be
       generated.

  c) GROUP PLAYOFF
     - Requires at least 4 players.
     - All players play a single round-robin group stage for the
       configured number of cycles.
     - Top 4 finishers advance to the semi-finals:
         Semi 1: 1st place vs 4th place
         Semi 2: 2nd place vs 3rd place
     - If the 4th- and 5th-placed players are tied on points, semi-finals
       cannot be generated until a 4th-place decider match is played
       between them. The decider's winner takes the 4th qualifying spot
       (overriding the plain goal-difference/head-to-head tiebreak). This
       decider match does not count toward the group table.

STANDINGS / POINTS (all group and round-robin formats):
     - Win = 3 points, Draw = 1 point each, Loss = 0 points.
     - Sort order: points (desc) → goal difference (desc) → goals scored
       (desc).
     - Any remaining tie (same points AND same goal difference) is broken
       by a head-to-head mini-league among just the tied players:
       head-to-head points → head-to-head goal difference → head-to-head
       goals scored → overall goals scored, in that order.

KNOCKOUT STAGE (group_knockout and group_playoff):
     - Semi-finals are two-legged ties (each pairing plays a home leg and
       an away leg). The winner is decided on aggregate goals across both
       legs.
     - If a semi-final tie is level on aggregate, a single penalty-decider
       match must be played to determine who advances.
     - The final is a single match between the two semi-final winners.
     - If the final ends in a draw, a penalty-decider match must be played
       to determine the champion.
     - The champion is whoever wins the final (or the final's penalty
       shootout) — even if they didn't top their group. Every other
       player keeps their group-stage table position for the final
       overall standings.

PRESTIGE WEIGHT (used by the player ranking system, see section 4):
     - Automatically derived from the number of players in the
       championship — not chosen by the admin:
         2–4 players  → "friendly" tier → weight 2
         5–6 players  → "standard" tier → weight 4
         7+  players  → "major"    tier → weight 8
     - A bigger field is treated as objectively harder to win outright, so
       the title counts for more toward a player's Legacy score.


4. PLAYER RANKING — "P4P" (Pound-for-Pound) SCORE
----------------------------------------------------
Each player gets a composite score from 0–100, built from eight weighted
pillars that sum to 100%:

     Win Quality           30%   Bayesian-shrunk win rate (win=1, draw=0.5,
                                  loss=0), pulled toward a neutral 50%
                                  baseline on small samples so a 2-0 record
                                  isn't scored as "100%".
     Legacy (titles)       20%   Prestige-weighted championship titles,
                                  with diminishing returns — a single
                                  "major" title alone reaches half of the
                                  maximum possible Legacy score.
     Experience            10%   Rewards total matches played, saturating
                                  gradually (still gaining meaningfully out
                                  to ~80–120 games, then plateauing so pure
                                  volume can't buy the top rank alone).
     Strength of Schedule  10%   Performance weighted by opponents'
                                  underlying skill (their own shrunk win
                                  rate), so beating strong opponents counts
                                  for more than beating weak ones.
     Recent Form           10%   Exponentially recency-weighted results
                                  with a 15-match half-life — no hard
                                  "last N games" cutoff to game.
     Goal Dominance         10%   Logistic-scaled average goal difference.
     Attack                 5%   Saturating reward for average goals
                                  scored per game.
     Defense                 5%   Saturating reward for LOW average goals
                                  conceded per game.

     - Six of the eight pillars (Win Quality, Strength of Schedule, Recent
       Form, Goal Dominance, Attack, Defense) are scaled down by a
       "confidence gate" when a player has few matches played, ramping up
       to near-full trust by roughly 20–30 games played. Legacy and
       Experience are exempt from this gate (Legacy is a verified,
       discrete achievement with no statistical noise; Experience already
       is the volume signal).
     - Goal Dominance, Attack, and Defense scale themselves to the current
       player pool's actual scoring behavior rather than using fixed
       constants, so they self-calibrate to however high- or low-scoring
       the matches on the platform tend to be.


5. ADMIN-CONFIGURABLE "GAMEPLAY RULES" NOTE
----------------------------------------------
- Admin Settings includes a freeform "Gameplay Rules" text field
  (Admin → Settings) intended for house rules, scoring conventions, or
  other platform-specific guidance to display to players.
- It is empty by default and can be edited by an admin at any time — any
  rules an admin has written there are not captured in this document since
  they live in the database, not the code.
